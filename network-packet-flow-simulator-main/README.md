# Network Packet Flow Simulator

> Free browser-based packet-flow visualization tool — watch packets traverse switches, routers, firewalls and see encapsulation in real time.

## 🚀 [**Launch Simulator →**](https://netsim.networkershome.com)

**Live at**: [https://netsim.networkershome.com](https://netsim.networkershome.com)
**Category**: Networking
**By**: [Networkers Home](https://www.networkershome.com) — free hands-on labs for the student community

---

## What you will learn

- Layer 2 MAC learning and STP behavior
- Layer 3 routing table lookups
- How NAT rewrites packet headers
- Firewall stateful inspection visualized
- Encapsulation at every OSI layer

---

## Ideal for these certifications

- CCNA 200-301
- Network+

---

## How to use it

1. Click **[Launch Simulator](https://netsim.networkershome.com)**
2. The simulator loads in your browser (desktop or tablet)
3. Work through the guided scenarios, or explore freely
4. No account. No email. No download.

---

## Why is this free?

Hands-on practice is the single biggest differentiator between passing an exam and actually being able to do the job. We believe practice should not be paywalled. So we keep these simulators open to everyone — students, career switchers, working professionals, and even competitor trainers.

If this tool helps you grow, please consider exploring our [full training programs](https://www.networkershome.com/courses) — that is how we sustain the free work.
